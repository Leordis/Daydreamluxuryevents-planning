The Visionary

Meet Adriana

With a background in fine arts and a passion for the effortless elegance of the Florida coast, I founded Daydream Luxury Events to bridge the gap between imagination and reality.

My philosophy is rooted in "Editorial Minimalism"—where every element serves a purpose and beauty is found in the intentional. I believe that a luxury event should feel personal, never staged, and leave a lasting impression of refined joy.

When I'm not scouting new garden venues in Palm Beach or selecting the perfect sage-toned silks, you'll find me at the beach with a sketchbook, drawing inspiration from the very landscapes that host my most beautiful celebrations.

Adriana V.
